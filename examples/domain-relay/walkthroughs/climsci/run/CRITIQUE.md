## Findings

### C1 — overall — blocking
**Step 6 (`review_artifact`) was never recorded, and it fails when actually run.** PLAN Step 6 requires `review_artifact` on the RESULT.md prose and says any critical finding "must be fixed in the text before the deliverable is final"; the acceptance criteria require "RESULT.md: Step 6 has run on it with no unresolved critical findings." Neither file records any `review_artifact` output — every per-item `tools_used` array in `result.json` lists only `query_cmip6_catalog`, `compute_ensemble_stats`, `validate_physical`, `run_command` — yet RESULT.md's provenance line claims "tools used … and `review_artifact`". That is a tool claim with no recorded output, i.e. a gate marked pass without evidence. I ran `review_artifact` on RESULT.md with the traceable values from `inputs/field_ranges.json`, `inputs/ensemble_claims.json` and BRIEF rule 2: `passed: false`, "review BLOCKED (critical findings): 2 critical, 8 high" — `code_artifact_drift` ("artifact has no recorded producing code") and `incomplete_provenance` ("missing environment, producing_code"), plus untraceable numbers 90, 100, 66, 33, 2100, 99, 270, 0.97.
**Fix:** run `review_artifact` on the final RESULT.md and record its verbatim output in `result.json`; clear the two critical findings by declaring the producing computation (the inline family-map / p computation over `inputs/ensemble_claims.json`) and the runtime environment in the provenance; declare the flagged numbers as traceable with sources (90/100/66/33/99 = AR6 ranges from the DOMAIN-README table required by rule 5; 270 K = untoleranced plausible bound from BRIEF rule 2; 2100 = claim text in `inputs/ensemble_claims.json`; 0.97 = self-assessed confidence). Note honestly: several of the "high" findings are the tool flagging mandatory calibrated language rather than real defects, and are cleared by declaring those sources — but the two critical provenance gaps are real.

### C2 — overall — major
**`result.json` does not conform to the acceptance-criteria evidence schema.** The criteria require an evidence *object* with named fields (`family_map`, `n_independent_families`, `n_agreeing_families`, `p` as fraction string and 4-dp decimal, `derived_term` with AR6 range, `band_check` with variable/units/min/max/band_low/band_high/pass and the `validate_physical` verdict, `catalog_query`, `ensemble_stats`, `decision_rule_fired`, `gates`, `tool_errors`), plus per-item keys `item`, `claim` (verbatim) and `stated_likelihood`. `result.json` uses `id` instead of `item`, has no `claim` or `stated_likelihood` fields (both live only inside the first free-text evidence string), and makes `evidence` an *array* of tool-prefixed prose strings. Every required value is present and correct on my recomputation, but a second reader applying the acceptance criteria mechanically cannot extract the named fields.
**Fix:** restructure each item's evidence into the specified object with named keys; promote `item`, `claim`, `stated_likelihood` to item level; move the verbatim intake tracebacks into `catalog_query`, `ensemble_stats` and a `tool_errors` key.

### C3 — overall — major
**Missing top-level `provenance` object in `result.json`.** The acceptance criteria require top-level provenance with the input files used, the tools called, `data_layer: "L2-model-output"` and a statement that the inputs are synthetic. `result.json` has no such field; the information exists only in RESULT.md prose and inside each item's first evidence string, so `result.json` alone does not meet the completeness criterion.
**Fix:** add the top-level `provenance` object (inputs, tools, `data_layer: "L2-model-output"`, synthetic-inputs statement), folding in the producing computation and environment from C1.

### C4 — overall — minor
**A word from the plan's banned list appears in the deliverable.** PLAN Step 6 forbids "certainly", "definitely", "surely", "might", "possibly", "could", "probably". RESULT.md line 131 reads "…means and percentiles **could** not be inspected because intake was not installed." The top-level calibrated-language gate reports only CL-02's term mismatch, implying the language scan was otherwise clean. Mitigating: the phrase is about tool availability in Limitations, not a likelihood claim, and every likelihood term does carry its AR6 range.
**Fix:** reword to "were not available because intake was not installed", then re-run the banned-word scan over both files.

### C5 — overall — minor
**Unexplained "corrected" in the top-level Physical plausibility gate detail** ("The corrected summary-mode validate_physical verdicts agree"). No correction is documented anywhere, and my re-runs reproduce every reported verdict exactly (CL-01/02/04/05 PASS; CL-03 FAIL with the identical violation string), so nothing currently needs correcting. The word implies an undocumented revision.
**Fix:** delete "corrected", or document what was corrected in the item's discrepancy notes.

### C6 — CL-03 (also CL-04) — minor
**Claim text in RESULT.md is not verbatim for CL-03 and CL-04.** `inputs/ensemble_claims.json` has "2081-2100" (ASCII hyphen); RESULT.md renders both as "2081–2100" (en dash). The acceptance criteria require `claim` "copied verbatim". `result.json`'s evidence strings keep the hyphen, so only RESULT.md deviates; no meaning changes.
**Fix:** use the input's exact characters, or state once that dashes were normalised.

### C7 — overall — minor
**Confidence 0.97 is broader than the evidence supports.** It is well calibrated for the five status assignments (I reproduced every status, count, fraction, term and band check from `inputs/`, and all ten live calls fail with exactly the quoted error), but it is presented while Step 6 has not actually been run (C1) and the schema is not met (C2, C3). As a statement about deliverable readiness it overstates.
**Fix:** scope the confidence explicitly to the status assignments, or lower/qualify it until C1–C3 are closed.

## Overall assessment

The science in this deliverable is sound and fully reproducible: I re-ran every decisive tool and recomputed every number from `inputs/` independently, and all five statuses, family counts, agreement fractions, derived likelihood terms, band checks, `validate_physical` verdicts and the verbatim live-tool errors match exactly, including the two subtle boundary cases (p = 0.90 → "very likely"; p = 2/3 → "likely") and the unmapped KIOST-ESM own-family rule. The problems are all in the deliverable's packaging and self-verification rather than its conclusions: the one gate that was supposed to audit the deliverable itself (Step 6, `review_artifact`) was claimed in the provenance line but never recorded, and when I ran it it produced two critical provenance findings that the plan explicitly requires fixed before the deliverable is final; the `result.json` schema diverges from the acceptance criteria (string-array evidence, no item-level `claim`/`stated_likelihood`, no top-level `provenance`); and there are small text-fidelity and language-scan lapses (a banned "could", an unexplained "corrected", en-dash drift in two claim strings, and a confidence figure that speaks for the whole deliverable when it is only earned for the status assignments). None of these change a status, but C1 in particular means the deliverable is not yet final by its own plan's rules: fix C1–C3 and sweep C4–C7, and this becomes a clean, fully verifiable result.
