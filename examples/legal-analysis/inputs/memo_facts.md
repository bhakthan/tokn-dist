# Synthetic facts (fictional parties)

Parties:
- Harbor Lane Supplies ("Harbor Lane"), a fictional buyer.
- Northfield Logistics ("Northfield"), a fictional carrier.

Background:
- In March, Northfield agreed to deliver 400 pallets to Harbor Lane by a fixed date.
- The contract sets a late-delivery penalty of 2% of the order value per day.
- Northfield delivered nine days late. Harbor Lane deducted the penalty from its
  invoice. Northfield says the delay was caused by a port strike, a force majeure
  event, and that the contract's force majeure clause does not list strikes.

Issue to analyse:
Whether a port strike that is not named in a force majeure clause excuses late
delivery under a contract that also contains a general "events beyond reasonable
control" phrase.

Note: these facts are invented for a demonstration of TOKN's legal gates.
