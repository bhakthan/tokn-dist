# Example Corp Duty of Care Standard

Owner: Corporate Security. Version 4, effective 1 March.

## Travel insurance registration

All **international** trips must be registered with the company travel
insurance provider before tickets are issued. Domestic trips are covered
automatically and need no registration.

## Security briefing

A pre-departure security briefing (30 minutes, online) is mandatory when either:

- the destination's government travel advisory is **Level 3 or higher**, or
- the trip includes overland travel between cities at night.

The briefing must be completed no more than 30 days before departure.

## Emergency contacts

Travellers keep the 24/7 assistance number in their phone and share their
itinerary with the Travel Desk. Itineraries are deleted 90 days after return.
