# Example Corp Travel Expenses (summary)

- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy above 6 hours.
- Hotel cap: the city rate published by the Travel Desk, or 180 per night where no rate is published.
- Meals are reimbursed against receipts up to the daily allowance for the destination city.
- Expense reports are due within 30 days of return.

This page does not cover approvals or safety requirements; see the travel policy
and the duty of care standard.
