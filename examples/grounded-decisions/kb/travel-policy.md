# Example Corp Business Travel Policy

Owner: Travel Desk. Applies to all employees and contractors travelling on company business.

## 1. Approval by government travel advisory level

Every international trip is approved according to the **current** government
travel advisory level for the destination country. Use the level published by
the traveller's home government on the day the trip is requested, not the level
at the time a previous trip was booked.

| Advisory level | Meaning | Approval required |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Exercise normal precautions | Line manager (automatic when booked 14 or more days ahead) |
| Level 2 | Exercise increased caution | Line manager |
| Level 3 | Reconsider travel | Vice President **and** a security briefing before departure |
| Level 4 | Do not travel | Prohibited. No exceptions through this policy |

If the advisory level cannot be confirmed, the request goes to the Travel Desk
for manual review. Do not assume Level 1.

## 2. Booking window

Flights and hotels must be booked at least **14 calendar days** before departure.
A trip booked inside 14 days needs line-manager approval even at Level 1, and the
traveller must state the business reason for the short notice.

## 3. Purpose

Customer meetings, conferences where the employee presents, and internal
quarterly planning are business-essential. Optional training and
attend-only conferences are discretionary and can be declined during budget freezes.
