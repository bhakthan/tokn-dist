Run an autodesign experiment on the landing page in this folder.

Read design_brief.md first. It defines the goals, the files you may change, the four weighted dimensions, the seven binary gates, the evaluation command, and the budget.

Then improve landing/index.html and landing/style.css. Use the gate results and dimension scores from the evaluator to decide what to change next. Keep the product story and the call to action. Do not add external resources.

Each experiment should make one focused change. Stop when the budget is used up or no further experiment improves the frontier.
