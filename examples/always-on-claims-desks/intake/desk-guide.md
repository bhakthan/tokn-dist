# Intake desk guide: referral triggers (synthetic)

Refer a claim to the coverage-review desk when ANY trigger applies:

- [R-1] Late notice: reported more than 14 days after the date of loss, or
  the date of loss or discovery is uncertain.
- [R-2] The policyholder mentions earlier damage or a repair at the same
  location.
- [R-3] The loss date is within 60 days of the policy's first inception.
- [R-4] The estimate exceeds 25,000.
- [R-5] The reported cause suggests gradual leakage rather than a sudden event.

When no trigger applies, finish the claim yourself: no referral.
