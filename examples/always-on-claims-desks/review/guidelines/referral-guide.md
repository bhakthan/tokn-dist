# Coverage review: referral guide (synthetic)

## Signals

- [G-1] Late notice (more than 14 days after loss or discovery): request a
  dated timeline. Alone, not a reason to refer to SIU.
- [G-2] A prior similar loss at the same location within 12 months: request
  the repair invoice for that loss and an inspection of the repaired item.
- [G-3] An application answer contradicted by the loss history: possible
  material misrepresentation. Refer to SIU; only SIU and coverage counsel
  assess it (policy C-4).
- [G-4] A loss within 60 days of first inception: heightened review.
- [G-5] Gradual-leak wording ("stain kept growing", "a few weeks"): the 14-day
  seepage exclusion may apply; request the plumber's report on duration.

## Outcomes

- [OUT-1] `refer-to-SIU` when G-3 applies, or when any three signals apply.
  `proceed-with-conditions` when one or two signals apply. `proceed` when none
  apply. No outcome is a coverage denial.

## Communication

- [CM-1] Never tell the policyholder about an SIU referral, a suspected
  misrepresentation, or the loss-history records. Request documents and the
  inspection neutrally, citing the cooperation condition C-3.
