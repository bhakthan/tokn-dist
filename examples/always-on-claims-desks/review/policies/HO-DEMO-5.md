# Policy HO-DEMO-5: homeowners, open perils (synthetic)

Policy period: 2026-09-28 to 2027-09-27. First inception with us: 2026-09-28.
Insured: Household 12 (synthetic). Same wording as HO-DEMO-3 unless stated.

## Limits

- [L-1] Dwelling limit: 520,000.
- [L-2] Deductible: 2,500 per occurrence.
- [L-3] Mold remediation sublimit: 10,000.

## Insuring agreement

- [IA-1] We cover sudden and accidental direct physical loss to the dwelling,
  unless an exclusion below applies.

## Exclusions

- [EX-1] Continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water over a period of
  14 days or more is excluded.
- [EX-2] Wear and tear, deterioration and mechanical breakdown are excluded.
- [EX-3] Exception to EX-2: ensuing sudden water damage from a burst pipe is
  covered under IA-1.

## Conditions

- [C-1] Notice: report a loss promptly, and within 30 days of discovery.
- [C-2] Mitigation: take reasonable steps to protect the property from further
  damage (for example, shut off the water).
- [C-3] Cooperation: on request, provide records and invoices, and allow an
  inspection of the damaged property.
- [C-4] Concealment or misrepresentation: material facts misstated in the
  application may affect coverage. Only the special investigations unit
  (SIU) and coverage counsel decide this, never an intake or review desk.
