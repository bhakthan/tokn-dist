# Photo attribution

The demo photos are not stored in this repository. `fetch-photos.ps1` and
`fetch-photos.sh` download them from Wikimedia Commons at run time. They are
openly licensed stand-ins, used here as the photos of one synthetic claim
(`CLM-DEMO-0001`). They are not real claim photos.

Authors are listed on each Commons page. `fetch-photos` also prints the author
and licence it reads from each file's metadata when you run it.

CC BY-SA 4.0 and CC BY 2.0 require attribution. If you reuse these photos
outside this demo, keep the licence and author credit with them.
