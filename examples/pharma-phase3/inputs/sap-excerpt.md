# Statistical Analysis Plan excerpt (synthetic, fictional)

Study VTX-417-P3-01 (fictional). Version 2.0.

## 1. Primary endpoint
Change from baseline in total nasal symptom score at week 24.
Population: intention-to-treat (ITT). Test: two-sided, alpha = 0.05.

## 2. Key secondary endpoint
Proportion of responders at week 24 (score reduction of at least 50%).
Hierarchical testing applies: a secondary is tested only if the primary
is significant.

## 3. Subgroups
Only the subgroups listed in this SAP are pre-specified. Any other
subgroup is exploratory and may not support a green-light claim.

## 4. Biomarker
The eosinophil marker cTAG is a pre-specified exploratory biomarker. A
claim using it requires a qualified assay (validation report on file).

## 5. Data quality
Source data verification (SDV) must be full for every site that
contributes to a claim.

## 6. Blinding
All efficacy analyses use blinded data review.
