title: Pump station shutdown checklist
timestamp: 2025-11-04T08:12:00Z
---

Shut the pump station down in this order:

1. Confirm the wet well level is below the low-float mark.
2. Lock out the breaker at the motor control centre.
3. Close the discharge valve before the isolation valve.
4. Log the shutdown time in the operations log.
